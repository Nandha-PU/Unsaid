Say It Right: Help people say hard things in the right way
You need to message a professor, a recruiter, or a friend you let down. You know what you mean, but not how to say it, and one wrong tone changes the whole message.
Your mission: build a tool that helps people communicate what they mean, to someone specific, in a situation that matters.
Start here: a rough message goes in, and a better one comes out
Make it yours: Who is your user and what moment is stressful for them? How can your app teach them something, not just rewrite for them? What does "the right tone" really mean?
Tip: pick a niche. "Text rewriter" is common, but "first-year students emailing professors" is yours.
